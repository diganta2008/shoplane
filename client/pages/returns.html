Our promise

We stand behind everything we sell. If a product doesn't fit your life for any reason — the fit is off, the colour looks different in daylight, or you simply changed your mind — return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Pickup is free and paperwork is minimal.

How to return an item

  1. Open the order. Head to Account → Orders, find the order, and click Return item.
  2. Pick a reason. Choose the item(s), tell us why you're returning them, and select refund-to-original-payment or store credit (store credit lands instantly).
  3. Print & stick the label. We'll email you a pre-paid courier label within a few minutes. Print it, tape it on the outside of the box, and hand the package to our courier or drop it at any partner drop point.
  4. Track the return. The order page shows live status — In transit → Received → Refunded. You'll get an email at each step.
  5. Money in your account. Once inspected, refunds are issued within 24 hours to the original payment method (banks take 3 – 7 days to reflect it) or instantly as store credit.

What qualifies for a return?

✓ Fashion & apparel

Unworn, tags intact, within 30 days.

✓ Electronics & gadgets

Working, all accessories present, box in good condition.

✓ Home & kitchen

Unused, original packaging, within 30 days.

✓ Books

No writing, dog-ears or damage.

✗ Personal care & cosmetics

No returns once seals are broken (hygiene).

✗ Innerwear & swimwear

Non-returnable once worn or tags removed.

✗ Perishables & food

Not eligible for return.

✗ Custom / made-to-order

Non-refundable unless defective on arrival.

The exclusion, if any, is clearly called out under the buy-box on every product page and re-confirmed at checkout.

Refunds & timing

Refunds are processed within 24 hours of the returned item reaching our warehouse. Where the money lands depends on the original payment method:

  • UPI / net-banking: 1 – 3 business days.
  • Credit / debit card: 3 – 7 business days (bank-dependent).
  • Wallets: instant to 24 hours.
  • Cash on delivery: paid out to a bank account you nominate (we'll ask for the details during the return flow).
  • Store credit: instant, never expires.

Exchanges

Want a different size, colour or variant instead of a refund? Choose Exchange during the return flow. If the replacement costs more we'll ask you to pay the difference; if it costs less we'll refund the difference. The replacement ships as soon as we've received the original back.

Damaged, defective, or wrong item

If your package arrives damaged, or contains the wrong or a defective item, we cover it fully:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
  • We'll dispatch a replacement or issue a full refund — your choice.
  • Return pickup is free and immediate.
Keep the original packaging and, if possible, a couple of photos of the damage — it helps us route the claim to the courier faster.
