Orders & payments

Placing an order, changing it, and paying for it.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, RuPay), UPI, net-banking from 60+ Indian banks, wallets like Paytm and PhonePe, and buy-now-pay-later options from Simpl and LazyPay. All payments are processed over a PCI-DSS certified gateway.

Can I edit or cancel my order after placing it?

You can cancel any order for a full refund up until it's marked Shipped. Head to Account → Orders, open the order, and click Cancel. Address edits are possible only before the order enters processing (usually a 30-minute window).

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. We never store raw card numbers on our servers — payments go directly through our tokenised gateway. The site is served over HTTPS and passwords are stored using bcrypt with per-user salts.

Do you charge tax?

Prices shown are inclusive of GST. The exact tax breakdown is visible on your cart summary before you pay, and on the final invoice you receive by email after checkout.

Shipping & delivery

Quick answers — for the full breakdown see the shipping policy.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Standard delivery is 2 – 3 business days across metros and 4 – 6 days elsewhere. Expedited (next-day) delivery is available at checkout for serviceable pin codes.

Is shipping free?

Yes — on every order over ₹8,000 (approx. $100). Under that, a flat ₹99 shipping fee applies. Coupon FREESHIP waives the fee on eligible orders.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships you'll get an email with a tracking link. You can also view live status any time under Account → Orders.

Do you deliver internationally?

Not yet — we currently ship to Indian addresses only. International expansion is on our 2026 roadmap.

Returns & refunds

Short version below — full policy is on the returns page.

What's your return window?

30 days from delivery, no questions asked, on almost everything. Some hygiene-sensitive categories (opened cosmetics, innerwear) are excluded — the exclusion is called out on the product page.

How do I start a return?

Open the order in Account → Orders, tap Return item, pick a reason, and choose refund-to-original or a store credit. We'll email a pre-paid label to print and stick on the box; pickup is free.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds are processed within 24 hours of the item arriving at our warehouse. Depending on your bank, the money lands in 3 – 7 business days. Store credit is instant.

Can I exchange for a different size or colour?

Yes — during the return flow, pick Exchange instead of refund and choose the new variant. If the replacement costs more you'll be prompted to pay the difference; if it costs less we'll refund it.

Account & support

Logging in, changing details, and getting help.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

Click Forgot password? on the login page, enter the email attached to your account, and follow the reset link we send. Links are valid for 30 minutes.

Can I change my registered email address?

Yes — under Account → Profile. We'll send a verification link to the new address before switching over.

How do I delete my account?

Write to us at privacy@shoplane.demo from the email on file. We'll confirm and permanently delete your data within 30 days per the Privacy Policy.

How do I contact support?
