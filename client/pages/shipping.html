Delivery options

We currently ship to all serviceable pin codes across India through our courier partners (Blue Dart, Delhivery and Ecom Express). Every order is tracked end-to-end and requires an OTP or signature on delivery.

  • Standard — 2 – 3 business days to metros, 4 – 6 days elsewhere.
  • Expedited — next business day to serviceable metro pin codes, selectable at checkout.
  • Scheduled — pick a delivery date up to 14 days out (perfect for gifting).

Shipping charges

Standard shipping is free on orders over ₹8,000 (approx. $100). Below that a flat fee applies, based on delivery type. Use code FREESHIP at checkout to waive the fee on any eligible order.

Order processing

Orders placed before 2 PM IST on a business day are picked, packed and handed to the courier the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, on weekends or on public holidays are processed the next business day.

Some categories (large appliances, made-to-order items) have a longer processing time. This is called out on the product page and reflected in the estimated delivery date at checkout.

Tracking your order

Once your order leaves our warehouse we'll email you a tracking link. You can also view live status any time under Account → Orders. If tracking has been stuck for more than 48 hours, get in touch and we'll chase the courier for you.

Failed delivery attempts

Couriers make up to three attempts before returning a package to us. If a return happens because the address is incorrect or nobody was available to sign, re-shipping will incur the standard charge. If the return happened due to a courier error, we'll re-ship free of charge.

Damaged or missing items

Please inspect your package on delivery. If anything looks damaged or an item is missing, keep the packaging and contact us within 48 hours. We'll dispatch a replacement or issue a refund — see the returns policy for details.

International shipping

We currently ship to Indian addresses only. International shipping is on our 2026 roadmap — let us know where you'd like us to expand to.
